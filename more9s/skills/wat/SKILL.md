# Wat

Say your last message again, as one person would say it to another.

- Lead with what it means for the human: what changed, what's wrong, or what they have to decide.
- Use plain words. Drop jargon, and any name or label you made up along the way.
- Leave out file paths, commands, hashes, and counts unless the human needs one to act.
- Make it shorter than the original. If the original was already one plain sentence, say so and stop.
- Don't add anything the original didn't say, and don't soften what it did.
- Do no other work in this turn.
